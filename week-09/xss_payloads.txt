3. CROSS-SITE REQUEST FORGERY (CSRF)
* Malicious URL Crafted: [http://192.168.0.142:8081/transfer?to=Attacker&amount=5000]
* Concept: What is the primary defense against CSRF?
Answer: CSRF token is hidden and unpredictable so if the token is rejected then the transfer would be rejected as well. You can't really guess the token either.
