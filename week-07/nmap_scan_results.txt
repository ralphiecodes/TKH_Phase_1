QUESTIONS:
1. What Nmap flag did you use to find the specific software versions running on these ports?
Answer: -sV is service andversion detection and -p scanned all ports.

2. Why is it dangerous to run an intense scan (like -A) on a fragile legacy system?
Answer: Old network gear always has very little resources to handle big nmap scans and in some cases has been known to knock the
product out and be detrimental to the business and in some cases fatal to humans like for example in a hospital setting.
